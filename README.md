# PokeSurvivor Plus

**A free browser extension that makes [PokéSurvivor](https://pokesurvivor.com/) better:**<br>
Endless runs, co-op rooms of up to 8 with room codes, a redesigned HUD, a damage meter, a minimap, full controller support, co-op chat & pings, Spanish, and dozens of quality-of-life upgrades.

## Install

### From the Chrome Web Store (recommended)

**[➕ Get PokeSurvivor Plus on the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/pokesurvivor-plus/deobgjkejfjknofekpfccfbghgbhiidn)**

1. Open the [store page](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/pokesurvivor-plus/deobgjkejfjknofekpfccfbghgbhiidn) and click **Add to Chrome**, then **Add extension**.
2. Open or reload [pokesurvivor.com](https://pokesurvivor.com/). All options are in the game's **Settings**.

Updates install automatically. The store page also works in **Edge**, **Brave** and **Opera** (see [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md) for the one-time setting Edge and Opera need).

### Manual install (zip)

Prefer to load it yourself, or want a release before it reaches the store? Download **`pokesurvivor-plus-1.5.0.zip`** from the [latest release](https://github.com/PokeSurvivorPlus/PokeSurvivorPlus/releases/latest), extract it, open your browser's extensions page, turn on **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked** and pick the extracted folder. Don't keep both versions switched on at once.

Works in **Chromium-based desktop browsers** (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera…). Firefox, Safari and mobile aren't supported yet. Step-by-step help, updating and troubleshooting: **[INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md)**.

## Is it fair to the game?

Yes. PokeSurvivor Plus changes how the game looks and feels, not what's sent to the leaderboard. Endless runs are never registered with or sent to the leaderboard, unlock no achievements and leave your game stats exactly as they were. Everything else is interface, information and quality of life.

## Privacy

PokeSurvivor Plus has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and sends nothing to its developer. Your settings and run history stay in your browser. It only contacts [survivortiers.pages.dev](https://survivortiers.pages.dev/en/) (if you turn on tier letters) and SpriteCollab on GitHub (Pokémon portraits). Co-op extras (chat, pings, damage numbers) travel between players over the game's own connection. Full details: **[Privacy policy](https://pokesurvivorplus.github.io/PokeSurvivor-Plus-Privacy/)**.
