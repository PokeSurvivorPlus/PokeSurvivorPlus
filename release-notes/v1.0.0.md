# PokeSurvivor Plus 1.0.0

The first full release of PokeSurvivor Plus, a free browser extension for [PokéSurvivor](https://pokesurvivor.com/).

## ⬇ Install

1. Download **`pokesurvivor-plus-1.0.0.zip`** below (under **Assets**) and extract it somewhere permanent.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://`, `brave://`, `opera://extensions`), turn on **Developer mode** and click **Load unpacked**.
3. Select the **`pokesurvivor-plus-1.0.0`** folder and reload pokesurvivor.com.

Chromium-based desktop browsers only for now. Chrome Web Store: coming soon. Full guide: [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/PokeSurvivorPlus/PokeSurvivorPlus/blob/main/INSTALL.md).

## ✨ New in 1.0

- **∞ Endless runs.** The big purple Endless button under Play starts a run with no Judgment at 30:00: from minute 60 enemies toughen faster and faster, rifts keep opening and a boss comes every 3 minutes. Summon Arceus from the pause menu to end it (the host, in co-op). Endless runs are never sent to the leaderboard, unlock no achievements and leave your game stats and best time as they were; they keep their own records in Stats.
- **Controller prompts built into every window**, a cursor that stays inside the open window, LB / RB (or Q / E) to switch tabs everywhere — including Stats — and LT / RT for the leaderboard's Solo / Group.
- **Log out from your profile** (click your name at the top).
- **Shiny search:** type “shiny” on the Pokémon screen to see every Pokémon whose shiny you have, shown as shinies.
- **Three more boss themes**, chat and ping sounds, weather tooltips, and the Pokémon wandering behind the menu are sometimes shiny.
- **Much stronger colour blind mode**, tuned on the game's own colours.

## 🔧 Fixes

- The Pokémon window's region tabs are full size again, and Pokémon in the National list no longer stay grey balls after switching regions.
- The Pokémon window keeps the same size whichever Pokémon you pick.
- The share card, What's new and your profile favourites can be used with a controller.
