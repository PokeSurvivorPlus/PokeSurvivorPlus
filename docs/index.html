What you get

Everything lives inside the game's own Settings screen, and almost all of it can be switched off.

New in 1.0

∞ Endless runs

No Judgment at 30:00. Keep surviving as enemies grow stronger and stronger, with rifts that keep opening and a boss every 3 minutes. Summon Arceus from the pause menu when you're ready to end it.

  • Never sent to the leaderboard, no achievements, your game stats untouched
  • Separate endless records, with an ∞ badge on runs and share cards
  • Works in co-op (the host starts it and can summon Arceus)

A redesigned HUD & damage meter

Team cards, a boss plate whose portrait winces when hit, arrows to teammates, bosses, rifts and treasure thieves, and a clock panel with biome, kills, coins and tickets.

  • Hold Tab for damage, damage per second and your upgrades
  • A draggable, resizable minimap with toggleable markers
  • Tooltips on moves, items and weather; scale it from 1× to 2×

Co-op that feels like a team

Talk and point without leaving the game. Everything travels over the game's own connection between players.

  • Ping wheel: Help, Boss, Chest, Here — with sounds and arrows
  • Chat with bubbles over players, and in the room on the main menu
  • Pause for everyone, spectate while down, fewer disconnects

Play it on a controller

Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro and Steam controllers, with real button prompts built into every window.

  • A menu cursor for every screen, LB / RB to switch tabs
  • Every button rebindable, plus rumble when you're hit
  • Pick level-up cards straight from X / Y / B

A smarter Pokémon screen

Find any Pokémon in seconds and see what it can really do.

  • National tab, search (type shiny for your shinies), sorting and combined filters
  • Favourites, personal records and every move with its details
  • Optional tier letters from the community list SurvivorTiers

Stats & share cards

Relive your best runs and show them off.

  • Your last 30 runs with damage-over-time graphs and top attacks
  • Records for every Pokémon, announced when you beat them
  • One click for a share card image to post in your group chat

Install in two minutes

Chrome Web Store: coming soon. Until then, load it by hand:

  1. 1

    Download

    Get pokesurvivor-plus-1.0.0.zip from the latest release and extract it somewhere you'll keep it.

  2. 2

    Open extensions

    Type chrome://extensions in the address bar and turn on Developer mode.

  3. 3

    Load unpacked

    Click Load unpacked, pick the pokesurvivor-plus-1.0.0 folder, then open pokesurvivor.com.

Updating, uninstalling and troubleshooting: full install guide. Firefox, Safari and mobile browsers aren't supported yet.

⚖️ Fair to the game

PokeSurvivor Plus changes how the game looks and feels, not what reaches the leaderboard. Endless runs are never registered or submitted, unlock no achievements and leave your stats exactly as they were.

🔒 Private by design

No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and nothing is sent to the developer. Settings and run history stay in your browser. Privacy policy

FAQ

Is PokeSurvivor Plus free?

Yes, completely: no ads, no purchases, no accounts. It's an open-source fan project.

Is it made by PokéSurvivor's developer?

No. PokeSurvivor Plus is an unofficial extension by ABSLS. PokéSurvivor is a separate fan game by JPTER78. Please report extension problems on GitHub, not to the game's developer.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chromium-based desktop browsers such as Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera. Firefox, Safari and mobile browsers aren't supported yet.

Why does my browser mention “developer mode”?

Extensions loaded by hand (rather than from a store) need developer mode turned on. That's expected until PokeSurvivor Plus is on the Chrome Web Store.

Will my progress or settings be lost when I update?

No. Your settings, records and run history are saved in your browser on pokesurvivor.com, not in the extension folder. You can also export a backup from Settings → Extras → Backup.

Something stopped working after a game update.

PokeSurvivor Plus checks each feature against the game and switches off just the part that broke. Open Settings → Extras → Status to see which, check for a newer release, and report it.

Do my friends need it for co-op?

No, you can play with anyone. Chat, pings, shared damage numbers and voting to pause need PokeSurvivor Plus, and work best when the host has it too.
